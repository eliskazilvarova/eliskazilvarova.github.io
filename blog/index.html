One Way Ticket 1

Place: Vienna Airport It is finally here - the big journey! Unfortunately, unlike Tomas, who had a whole gap year for his journey, I have only about 14 days for mine :) But I’ll try to make the most of it. Starting strong with my first night at the airport in Vienna. Little did I know that Vienna airport does actually have multiple terminal, and when you buy the lowcost plane ticket, you are also going to get the lowcost airport :D So, when I arrived by train to the airport, I thought that I will be spending the night in some cozy corner of the big, shiny and modern Vienna airport. Instead of it, I am sitting in “Terminal 1”, which consists of 15 chairs, 3 closed shops, and too many people waiting probably for the same plane as I am (many of them unfortunately for me, barefoot…). My plane is in 6 hours ish, so I guess I’ll try to cope with the conditions and I’ll survive until the morning - Bilbao is waiting for me!
