About me

A junior developer and data analyst with professional experience in digital marketing and website management. I combine a creative approach with a strong analytical mindset to build and improve digital solutions.

My path to the world of technology is driven by a fascination with how data and well-crafted digital tools can solve human challenges. With a background in digital marketing, and development at OUSHI UPOL, I’ve had the chance to work on both sides: understanding user needs and building the solutions to meet them.

I thrive on turning ideas into reality. Whether I am structuring a project’s chaos into a clear roadmap, or extracting actionable insights from data using Python, I am dedicated to turning complex challenges into concrete results.

In fact, this very website is one of my recent projects—a hands-on exercise in learning static site generation with Hugo, managing code with Git, and deploying via GitHub Pages. It’s a testament to my commitment to continuous learning and practical application of new skills.

My formal education in psychotherapy complements my technical skills, giving me a unique perspective on user experience and effective communication. When I’m not coding or exploring data, you can usually find me rock climbing, backpacking, or planning my next trip.
